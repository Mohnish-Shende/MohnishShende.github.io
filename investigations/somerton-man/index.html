Investigative Hypotheses & Models

Hypothesis 1

Toxicological profile suggests acute cardiac failure induced by rapidly metabolizing digitalis-type or gitalin-family glycoside rather than synthetic organophosphate.

Hypothesis 2

Cold War intelligence context (Woomera rocket range proximity, micro-film apparatus, hidden cipher sequence) indicates covert operational involvement rather than simple vagrancy.

Hypothesis 3

Tamám Shud torn slip and Rubáiyát edition represent deliberate operational signaling or personal contact verification.

Uncertainty Boundaries

Bounded Conclusions

Bounded Conclusion 1

Established case facts confirm the death of an unidentified male on December 1, 1948, with non-specific organ congestion and no external signs of violence.

Bounded Conclusion 2

Analytical observations confirm the presence of unlabelled European garments and a micro-printed Persian poetry slip matching a specific 1941 edition.

Bounded Conclusion 3

Offender identity and exact cause of death remain bounded by available historical evidence; modern DNA leads remain subject to corroborative documentation.
