Purpose

What the application does

The application allows a user-authorized Notion workflow or agent to authenticate with Google and request Google Cloud functionality through Google’s Cloud CLI MCP service. Access is subject to the authorizing Google account, its Google Cloud permissions, and the operations the user directs through the workflow.

This is a personal integration. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or owned by Google or Notion. Google, Google Cloud, and Notion are trademarks of their respective owners.

Privacy

How authorization and data are handled

The privacy policy identifies the requested Google OAuth scope, explains the intended use of Google Cloud data and credentials, preserves the limits of what is known about runtime storage and logging, and describes how to revoke access.
